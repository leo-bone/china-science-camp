# China Science Camp

Premium science and technology immersion camps in China for Middle Eastern families.

🌐 **Live Site**: https://chinasciencecamp.uichain.org

## About

China Science Camp offers world-class tech immersion experiences for families from GCC countries (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain). Our programs combine visits to leading Chinese tech companies (DJI, Huawei, BYD, Tencent) with top university experiences and cultural exploration.

## Features

- ✅ Visa-free entry for all GCC citizens
- ✅ 100% Halal-friendly service
- ✅ Business-class travel & 5-star accommodation
- ✅ Private family tours (no mixed groups)
- ✅ English-speaking guides
- ✅ Customizable itineraries
