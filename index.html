FAQ

Common Questions from Parents

No visa required! Citizens of UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kuwait, and Bahrain enjoy visa-free entry to China for up to 30 days. We assist with all arrival documentation and fast-track clearance.
We partner exclusively with certified Halal restaurants and hotels with Halal-certified kitchens. Our team pre-verifies every meal location. No pork, no alcohol, and no cross-contamination — guaranteed. We also provide a list of certified restaurants to families in advance.
Our programs are designed for families with children aged 8-18. Activities are interactive (not just passive touring) — drone flying, robot programming, EV factory tours — so children stay engaged throughout. We can also tailor content for university-age students.
Absolutely. Every family is different. Want more university focus? More tech company visits? Shopping days? We design a fully customized itinerary after a free consultation call. Many families also add business matching sessions if the parents have industry interests.
Spring (Mar-May) and Autumn (Sep-Nov) are ideal — mild weather, clear skies, and tech companies are in full operation. Summer (Jul-Aug) is also popular for school breaks, though hotter. We do not operate during Ramadan daylight hours for obviously reasons — we adjust the itinerary to respect fasting schedules.
We recommend booking 2-3 months in advance for peak seasons (spring/autumn) to secure business-class seats and 5★ hotel rooms. For summer, 3-4 months ahead is safer. That said, we've pulled off arrangements in 3 weeks — just ask!
